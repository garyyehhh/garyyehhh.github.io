## Main idea

What was the central claim, result, or perspective?

## What surprised me

Record the part that changed how you think or exposed a gap in your understanding.

## Connection to my learning

How does this relate to a course, project, paper, or research direction?

## Follow-up questions

- What should I read next?
- Is there a small experiment I can run?
- What background do I need to understand this more deeply?
